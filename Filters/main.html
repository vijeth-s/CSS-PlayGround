CSS Filters

How to use this page

  1. Upload an image to start applying CSS filters.
  2. Move the sliders to adjust blur, brightness, contrast, grayscale, and more.
  3. Preview the changes, then download or copy the generated CSS.
How to use this page:
  • Blur (blur()): softens the image by spreading pixels outward.
  • Brightness (brightness()): makes the image lighter or darker.
  • Contrast (contrast()): increases or decreases the difference between light and dark areas.
  • Grayscale (grayscale()): converts the image to black and white.
  • Hue-Rotate (hue-rotate()): shifts the color tones around the color wheel.
  • Saturate (saturate()): boosts or reduces the intensity of colors.
  • Sepia (sepia()): gives the image a warm brown, old-photo effect.
  • Opacity (opacity()): makes the image more or less transparent.
  • Invert (invert()): flips colors to their opposite values.
